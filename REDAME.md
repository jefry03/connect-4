## my project connect 4
## game story 
1.The game is played on a 6-row by 7-column grid

2.Players take turns dropping their colored discs

3.discs fall straight down, occupying the lowest available space

4.A player wins when they align 4 of their discs consecutively in any direction:
   * Horizontally↔️
   * Vertically  ↕️
   * Diagonally(Upwards or Downwards) ↗️ ↘️
   
   5.f all 42 spaces are filled and neither player has 4 discs in a row, the game ends in a draw.
